# Hello there

I'm Marinos, 19 years old, starting my second year at the Department of Informatics and Telecommunications of the University of the Peloponnese. I'm also the founder of [STAMX Tech](https://www.stamx.tech), a small technology company working on weather monitoring stations, IoT hardware and web applications.

I like building hardware and software, and combining the two to see real-life results. I'm still exploring the STEM field, my abilities, and how far I can go.

## What I've built so far

- **[YourLightAR](https://apps.shopify.com/yourlightar)** – a Shopify app that turns product photos into 3D models shoppers can view in AR. Live on the Shopify App Store.
- **[University announcements bot](https://github.com/MarStamSudo/aegean-overwatch)** – a small Python script that sends my department's announcements to Discord.
- **[Agricultural weather station](https://github.com/MarStamSudo/Smart_AgroTech_ESP32)** – an ESP32-based station for agriculture that sends its readings in real time.
- **[ARspace](https://github.com/MarStamSudo/ARspace)** – a no-code WebAR platform for museums. Finished.

And many more.
